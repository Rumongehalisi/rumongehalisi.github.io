URAI

Research, Analysis & Innovation Unit: reliable data for projects that change lives.

Mission

Produce reliable knowledge, develop relevant analyses, promote innovative solutions and support the design of structured projects for the members and the Rumonge community.

URAI is an internal technical structure under the authority of the Executive Committee. Its creation document is under review and will then go to the General Assembly.

Four divisions

🔬

Research & Studies

Surveys, diagnostics and studies.

📊

Analysis & Data

Statistics and dashboards.

💡

Innovation & Development

Local solutions and pilot projects.

📝

Projects & Resource Mobilisation

Project design and partnerships.

Five strategic axes

AXIS 1

Research & knowledge

Socio-economic studies, surveys, participatory diagnostics, capitalisation.

AXIS 2

Data & decision support

Databases, dashboards, indicators and recommendations.

AXIS 3

Innovation

Local solutions, creativity workshops, pilot projects, good practices.

AXIS 4

Project development

Concept notes, feasibility, logical frameworks, budgets, portfolio.

AXIS 5

Resources & partnerships

Calls for proposals, applications, relations with partners.

Services to Burundian associations

On written request, URAI offers advice, technical support and capacity building: studies and diagnostics, monitoring and evaluation, project design and management, governance, administrative and financial management, resource mobilisation, training.

Our field surveys respect informed consent, confidentiality and anonymity of respondents.
