About

Who we are, our legal framework and our organisation.

Our association

The Rumonge Halisi Association is a non-profit, apolitical association governed by Law no. 1/02 of 27 January 2017 on non-profit associations. Created for an unlimited period, it is based in Centre Urbain hill, Zone, Commune and Province of Rumonge, Burundi, and brings together more than 100 members.

Our statutes are being adopted: the draft has been submitted to the General Assembly.

Governance

General Assembly – supreme body
Executive Committee – 5 members
Supervisory Council – 3 members
URAI – Technical unit
🏛️

General Assembly

Made up of all active members, it approves reports, accounts and budget, and elects the bodies. It meets twice a year.

🧑‍💼

Executive Committee

President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer and Adviser, elected for 5 years, renewable once. It runs the association day to day.

🔍

Supervisory Council

Three members elected for 2 years. It audits the accounts at least once a year and reports to the General Assembly.

Our members

Four categories: founding members, adhering members, honorary members and sympathisers. Founding and adhering members are the active members.
