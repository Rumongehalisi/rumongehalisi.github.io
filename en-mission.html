Mission

What we do for the Rumonge community.

Our priorities

🤝

Mutual aid in bereavement

Supporting members and their families from the declaration of death to burial or repatriation: paperwork, vigil, religious ceremony, material, moral and, where relevant, financial support.

❤️

Support for the sick

Visits, psychosocial support, help with access to care, tests, medicines and hospital stays, referral to health facilities, prevention.

Our six fields of action

📖

Education & Training

Schooling for orphans, higher and university education, vocational training.

➕

Health & Social Support

Visits, psychosocial support, help with access to care, tests and medicines, prevention.

🌱

Fight against Poverty

Youth micro-projects, income-generating activities, food aid, fight against malnutrition.

🤝

Solidarity & Mutual Aid

From death to burial or repatriation: paperwork, vigil, ceremony, material and moral support.

🕊️

Peace & Citizenship

Humanity, solidarity, patriotism, peaceful coexistence and safeguarding of peace.

📈

Community Development

Strengthening community action, advice to associations and research through URAI (Research, Analysis & Innovation Unit).

Other commitments

  • Helping orphans go to school
  • Integrating young mothers through vocational training
  • Helping widows in great distress and street children
  • Fighting malnutrition, notably through the KOKORIKO project
  • Promoting income-generating activities for unemployed youth
  • Raising youth awareness on sexually transmitted infections, including HIV/AIDS
  • Supporting youth socio-cultural and artistic activities
  • Building partnerships with NGOs, associations, foundations and agencies
In health, the association provides financial, material, moral and referral support; it performs no medical acts. Its activities in regulated fields (health, education, training) are carried out only after the required authorisations.

Solidarity Fund

A separate fund, supplied by members’ solidarity contributions, earmarked gifts and activity income, finances bereavement aid and support for the sick. It is managed separately and reported to the General Assembly each year. Beneficiaries’ health information remains confidential.

Fund aid is solidarity support granted according to available resources; it is neither insurance nor a guaranteed benefit.
