Privacy policy

How we handle your data.

Data collected

This site uses no tracking cookies and no analytics tool. The information you enter in the contact form (name, email address, phone number if you provide one, message) is sent only when you submit the form.

How messages are sent

Messages are sent through a technical provider (Formspree) and then to our email address. They are used only to reply to you.

Health data

Health information about the people we help stays confidential and is never published.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is not shared with anyone other than the technical provider above, which is needed to deliver the form.
