Vision

Where we want to go.

Our vision

To help form and promote humanity, solidarity and patriotism among the Burundian population, including through auxiliary services such as mutual aid, preaching, peaceful coexistence, the fight against unemployment, the safeguarding of peace and vocational training.

Any profit from our income-generating activities is used exclusively for our objectives; no profit is distributed to members.
